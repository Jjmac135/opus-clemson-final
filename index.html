Public service isn't something you do. It's something you build over a lifetime.

Opus exists to catch students early, before they've missed the window or assumed these careers weren't for them. It pairs each fellow with mentorship, real deliverables, and a two-year runway long enough to turn ambition into a body of work worth showing for it.
